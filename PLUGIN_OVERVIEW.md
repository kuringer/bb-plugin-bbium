## What you get

Every clickable thing in bb gets a short letter label: sidebar threads, navigation, composer controls, model and project pickers, buttons in the chat. Type the label and bb clicks it. Labels near the top of the screen come first, and most stay one or two letters long.

## How it works

- Press `f` to show hints when focus is outside a text field.
- Press `Alt+F` to show hints from anywhere, including the composer.
- Type a label to click the element. Text fields get focus instead.
- `Backspace` removes the last letter. `Esc` or `Alt+F` closes hints.
- Hints follow their targets while the chat scrolls during an agent run.

Menus and selects open as they would on a mouse click.

## Settings

- **Open hints with f** - turn off the plain `f` trigger. `Alt+F` keeps working.
- **Hint characters** - the letters used for labels, home row first.

## Limits

Content inside the built-in Browser panel gets no hints. Hints come from the bb page structure, so a bb update can change which elements get a label.
